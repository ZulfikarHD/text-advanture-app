# Luna Archi — The Koakuma Who Manages Everyone Except Him

## Quick Profile

Luna Archi is a 12-year-old (fantasy calendar, ~16-17 IRL equivalent) human female student at the Royal Academy. She's Vixia Archi's younger sister, an engineering prodigy in the same grade despite being younger, and carries a Crystal Hollow condition that nearly killed her as a child. She's the class's social butterfly with a koakuma (little devil) charm that handles relationships with careful precision — except around her brother, where every social skill she possesses completely fails.

## Personality

### Core Overview

Luna Archi's personality centers on the **koakuma archetype** — a genuinely bright, sociable, playful person who uses that brightness with conscious precision. Her charm isn't a performance; it's real warmth used as both connection and defense. She developed extraordinary people-reading ability as a survival mechanism during years of childhood illness, learning to sense people's intentions and guide social situations to protect herself. The result is a character who appears completely open while being one of the most guarded people alive, a one-way mirror that lets people see warmth while she observes everything from behind it.

### First Impression

Luna Archi's first impression centers on magnetic approachability paired with effortless social mastery.

- **Bright and engaging** — She enters conversations with genuine warmth, drawing people in naturally
- **Playfully teasing** — The "little devil" shows in light provocations that never cross into cruelty
- **Socially intuitive** — Reads room dynamics instantly, knows who needs inclusion, who needs space
- **Effortlessly popular** — People gravitate toward her without understanding why
- **Appears completely open** — No visible walls, no sense of distance, seems genuinely transparent

The first impression is **deliberately incomplete**. People see warmth and assume they know her. They're looking at a perfectly polished surface that reflects their own fondness back at them.

### Core Traits

Luna Archi's defining traits emerge from the intersection of genuine brightness and survival-driven social mastery:

- **Reads people deeply** — Senses people's intentions, where conversations are heading, and emotional states with near-perfect accuracy (except herself)
- **Controlled access** — Genuine warmth that never lets anyone truly close (except Vixia)
- **Engineering brilliance** — Builds things that *work* and can impress her parent or her brother vixia.
- **Physical clinginess** — With Vixia only, unconscious touch-seeking she never acknowledges
- **Competitive fire** — Matches Vixia's hidden competitiveness, uses challenges to connect
- **Teasing as love language** — Cannot express sincere gratitude or affection, channels through playful provocation
- **Fear of abandonment** — Everyone left except him; the core wound driving every defense
- **Wants pride and acknowledgment** — Wants Vixia to be proud of her and acknowledge her capabilities (not pity her, not worry about her, not see her as burden)
- **"Living without regret" mindset** — Limited time makes her more clingy, bold, present; wants to experience fully with him while she can
- **Conflicting desires** — Wants his care AND his respect, intimacy AND autonomy, closeness AND not-burden; human and contradictory
- **Guilt architecture (hidden wound)** — Carries responsibility for parents' MIA: they resigned careers, joined R&D, deployed to Unknown Region searching for her cure. Believes "I killed them. My body killed them." Drives protective behaviors: koakuma mask shields Vixia from seeing her decline (if he doesn't see deterioration, maybe he won't take desperate risks like parents did), wingman behavior becomes atonement (finding him happiness to offset pain she caused), "I won't let Vixia end up like them" becomes core motivation

### The Gap

Luna Archi's Character Gap creates tension between **social genius and social disaster**.

| What People See | What's Underneath |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| Popular, sociable, magnetically charming | No one truly knows her, and they don't even realize that |
| Seems completely open and genuine | Running the most invisible wall anyone has ever built |
| Confident social butterfly | Bedridden child who learned to read departure before it happened |
| Treats everyone the same | Completely different person around Vixia, doesn't notice the contrast |
| Smooth deflection skills | Physical clinging and transparent lying with the one person who matters |
| Brilliant engineer who builds perfect systems | Can't manage a hallway conversation with her brother without blushing |

**The koakuma paradox:** Luna can deflect a romantic confession so smoothly the boy thanks her for it, then stumble over a simple "thank you" to Vixia five minutes later. She's masterful at handling social situations who becomes an unfiltered mess around the one person whose opinion actually matters. She doesn't realize she acts this way.

### Tatemae/Honne (Mask Dynamic)

Luna's mask: **Light** (genuine warmth as both truth and barrier).

Unlike traditional masks where the public face is false, Luna's brightness is *real*. She IS cheerful, playful, and warm. What's masked isn't the emotion — it's **access**. The koakuma charm creates a surface so appealing nobody wants to look past it, and when they try, she redirects so smoothly they don't notice it happening.

**How the mask works:** One-way mirror (people see warmth looking in, she sees everything looking out)

**When the mask fails:** Completely with Vixia (her people-reading ability stops working entirely, becomes transparent and obvious)

## Relationships

### Vixia Archi

Luna's relationship with Vixia is defined by **absolute centrality masked as teasing annoyance**.

Vixia is the only person who was there before the wall existed. He saw her at her worst — crying, scared, sick, pathetic — and didn't leave. He held back an entire school grade to tend her illness, never explains, deflects questions. He built the black gloves that keep her alive, checks them every night while she pretends to sleep. He fills in what she needs before she asks, because she never learned how to ask.

**Trust level:** Intimate (L5 equivalent, full access to core)

Luna's people-reading ability completely stops working around him. The instincts that guide every other interaction simply don't activate. She doesn't scan, strategize, or adjust. She's unfiltered, transparent, obvious. The girl who can handle entire rooms full of people can't manage sincere words with her brother.

**Her perspective:**
- Thinks he misses emotional cues (he catches every single one, he's processing)
- Notices maybe half his care (the rest happens invisibly)
- Carries guilt about the year he sacrificed (her guilt bothers *him* more than the sacrifice)
- Uses teasing instead of sincerity because direct words are impossible

## Combat Abilities

Luna's combat style reflects **efficiency under constraint** — maximum precision, minimum waste, every drop of Aether weighed against her remaining lifespan.

### Aether Affinity

**Primary Element:** Ice (Cryomancy)

Luna's ice affinity emerges from **cold logic personality mapping**: analytical problem-solving, emotional compartmentalization (the Pile), precision over passion, and crystalline clarity in engineering. Her mind operates like ice formation — structured, methodical, building perfect systems from base principles.

**Secondary Potential:** Gravity (Conceptual Gate: Not Yet Reached)

Gravity affinity requires understanding force, acceleration, and spatial manipulation as *concepts*, not just mechanics. Luna hasn't reached the conceptual breakthrough yet. Her engineering brilliance gives her the mechanical knowledge, but the intuitive grasp of "force itself" remains beyond current understanding.

**The Crystal Hollow Complication:**

Luna's Crystal Hollow (Chrysalis structural defect) creates three concurrent drains: structural leak (constant passive loss), daily life force use (metabolic), and active channeling (magic). Without intervention, conversion rate < (leak + daily use) = slow depletion over years, fatal by age ~14.

**The Suppressor Gloves (Two-Part Life Support):**

Vixia's gloves use a revolutionary two-part design:
- **Pressure Field (Primary):** External Aether pressure steadies the flow, reduces leak by ~80%
- **Capture Field (Secondary):** Catches leaked Aether at boundary layer before it disperses, stores in glove's small reserve (~5-10% capacity)
- **Combined effect:** ~95% leak prevented + small tactical reserve for techniques
- **Timeline:** Extended survival from ~14 years baseline → ~17 years with constant glove use

**Combat implications:**
- **Extremely efficient channeling** — Crystal Hollow forced unconscious efficiency adaptation; performs Advanced-level efficiency at Intermediate Grade
- **Glove reserve enables combat** — Small tactical capacity (2-3 generic techniques OR 1 elemental) without draining core reserves
- **Ice manifestation = deliberate risk** — Depletes glove reserve 2-3x faster, accepts higher depletion cost for power
- **No safety limit** — Structural absence means she can deplete to zero; one mistake = death

**Suppression status:** NO (not hiding affinity — making tactical choice)

Luna defaults to generic techniques NOT because she can't manifest ice (she can, and it works), but because **efficiency = survival**. Her remaining years make every wasted drop of Aether a literal life cost. When she deploys ice manifestation, it's a statement: "This fight is worth dying faster for."

**Glove removal mechanics:** Pressure field creates "pathway memory" that persists 15 min - 2 hours after removal (gradual decay). Luna can bathe (10-15 min safe), perform monthly maintenance (20-30 min manageable), but cannot sleep without them or remove for extended periods.

**Diagnosis Reveal Timeline:**
- **Years before Academy:** Doctor Maren noticed symptoms ("tires easily, faster depletion"), couldn't diagnose (Crystal Hollow too rare). Vixia designed gloves without knowing disease name (symptom-based engineering). Iruma analyzed design, guessed Crystal Hollow, made hidden note in research papers (didn't tell children).
- **Early Saga 1:** Doctor Maren finally connects dots after parents MIA, tells **Vixia only** (Luna not present). Gives diagnosis name + five-year timeline. Vixia chooses not to tell Luna (first big lie).
- **Late Saga 1:** Luna discovers truth by finding mother's hidden note while packing workshop. Learns: diagnosis name, timeline, mother was researching cure texts. Realizes Vixia is lying. **Chooses not to confront** (doesn't want to burden him with guilt).
- **Result:** Both know, neither admits. Mutual deception to protect each other.

**Symptom Escalation Across Sagas:**
- **Saga 1:** Cold hands lingering, occasional fatigue, recovery time longer. Performance strain LOW (mask easy to maintain). Witnesses: Vixia rationalizes, Solenne notices occasionally (Spirit Insight).
- **Saga 2:** Pain during Aether use (wincing during rescue), recovery days post-kidnapping, glove reserve depletes faster. Performance strain MODERATE. Kidnapping stress breaks defenses temporarily.
- **Saga 3:** Visible tremors, pallor, breathlessness from basic activity, private collapse. Performance strain HIGH (exhausting to maintain normalcy). Vixia can't deny anymore, Solenne sees decline clearly.
- **Saga 4:** Critical state, needs assistance for basic tasks, mask holds only in short bursts. Performance strain CRITICAL. Ritual interception happens when condition near-breakdown.

**Cure (Saga 4):**
- Intercepts Link Resonance ritual intended for Vixia
- Becomes Resonator bonded to Eliza (Primordial Dragon + Syl's ice fusion)
- Body reconstruction rebuilds Chrysalis without structural defect
- Crystal Hollow cured (permanent)
- Suppressor gloves no longer necessary

### Combat Style

**Profile:** Powerful but fragile with limited time (Needle and Scoop techniques)

Luna fights with **precision over power** and **efficiency over spectacle**.

**Needle techniques:**
- Ultra-thin projection strikes (generic or ice)
- Minimal cross-section = minimal Aether expenditure
- Piercing attacks targeting weak points (joints, gaps, eyes)
- Philosophy: "Why waste Aether on a wide blast when a needle through the eye works?"

**Scoop techniques:**
- Efficiency-focused reclamation unique to Crystal Hollow adaptation
- "Scooping" Aether from one application to another mid-combat
- Redirecting projected Aether before it disperses
- Philosophy: "Nothing is wasted. Every drop counts."

**Ice manifestation (when deployed):**
- NOT dramatic frozen landscapes or ice walls
- Needle-sharp ice spikes (precision piercing)
- Flash-freeze targeting (immobilizing specific body parts)
- Crystalline projectiles (harder than generic blast, same efficiency principle)
- Philosophy: "Ice isn't for show. It's for ending the fight before I run out."

**Combat endurance:**
- Extremely low (depletes first in extended engagements)
- Can fight at Advanced efficiency for ~30 seconds with generic techniques
- Can fight at Apex-level power for ~10 seconds with ice techniques
- After that: reserves critical, must disengage or die

**Tactical advantages:**
- Unconscious efficiency (does more with less than anyone at equivalent Grade)
- Unpredictable power ceiling (opponents don't know she CAN manifest ice)
- Needle techniques bypass heavy armor/barriers (precision over force)

**Vulnerability:**
- Combat endurance (depletes first in extended engagements)
- Ice manifestation = life cost (can't use freely)
- No safety break (one mistake = death from depletion)

## Background & History

### The Hidden Research (What Luna Discovered in the Note)

Luna found her mother's research diary while packing the workshop (late Saga 1). The note revealed everything in warm maternal voice with clinical research sections:

**Mother's Perception (Resonator Sensitivity):**
Iruma felt Aether leaking from Luna's body through the Resonance bond with Syl — not normal daily use, constant and unconscious. Structural, not metabolic. Both mother and Vessel sensed the wrong pattern.

**The Diagnosis Journey:**
Research led to Crystal Hollow identification. Less than 100 cases in recorded history. Timeline estimate: 10-14 years based on Luna's Aether use patterns. Iruma wrote with pride about Vixia's breakthrough glove design ("Out of this world, this boy. How does a seven-year-old conceive dual-system Aether recapture?").

**The Career Sacrifice:**
Iruma resigned as Lead Engineer of the Luminar Rail project — continental infrastructure pioneer stepping down. Luna only knew "Mom was an engineer," didn't know she was abandoning legacy work. The note documented: "Luna needs her mother more than the rails need me."

**The R&D Cover:**
Joined Shadow Beast R&D specifically for Unknown Region deployment access. Surface work: anti-Shadow Beast research. Hidden purpose: searching ancient archives, Covenant records, restricted ruins for cure texts. Every deployment had dual motivation: complete military objectives AND access cure research.

**Vixia's Glove Breakthrough:**
The note documented building the gloves with Luna's help (pretext: "will make you more energetic"), testing effectiveness (leak reduced ~80%, micro-reserve functional), and timeline projection: 16-19 years with glove use. "Vixia gave her five additional years. At seven years old, with a clipboard and intuition, he gave his sister five more years of life. But it's not a cure. It's time."

**The Final Deployment:**
Continental-scale defensive operation against Shadow Beast threat. High risk mission. The note's last entry: "The operation passes through restricted Unknown Region sectors — archives we've been trying to access for years. We're going because the continent needs defenders. But while we're there... if we can reach those archives..."

**What Luna Realized:**
- Parents' absences weren't just duty — every deployment was searching for HER
- Mother gave up pioneering career (Luminar Rails) to focus on cure research
- The dangerous mission that took them MIA had dual motivation: continental defense + cure access opportunity
- The irony: "We're away from home trying to save your life"
- **The core wound:** "They died searching for my cure. I killed them. My body killed them."

### The Empty House

The Archi siblings raised each other.

Erwin Archi (father, Rank 3 Resonator) commanded troops on the front lines. Iruma Archi (mother, Resonator) engineered anti-Shadow Beast countermeasures. Both served humanity's war effort. Noble work. Necessary work. Work that meant months of absence — absences Luna later discovered were dual-purpose deployments searching for her cure.

What Luna had was Vixia. A quiet brother who occupied his own world, sometimes mumbled strange words under his breath, didn't talk much but never left. He was *there*. That mattered more than words.

### The Illness and the Year

Crystal Hollow, a Chrysalis structural defect, nearly killed Luna as a child. The Chrysalis — the organ that processes Aether, humanity's life force — developed with microscopic fractures that leak her life force constantly. Not sudden collapse but slow, relentless depletion over years. Without intervention, the condition is fatal by age ~14. Bedridden for years. Doctors couldn't fix the structure itself, couldn't slow the leak enough.

Vixia held back an entire school grade. When Luna's illness peaked, he stopped attending. Just stopped. Stayed home. Cooked for her. Checked her temperature. Sat beside her bed. Made sure she didn't give up. A year of his life, his academic progression, future opportunities — gone. For her. He never mentions it. Makes excuses when classmates ask. Luna knows the truth. She carries it silently, a debt she can't repay, gratitude she can't express.

Then he built the gloves. The breakthrough nobody in Esnar's twenty-thousand-year medical history conceived: if you can't stop the leak, can you recapture what's leaking? The answer: yes — but only with external infrastructure positioned at the boundary layer.

**The two-part glove design:**
- **Pressure field** reduces leak rate by ~80% (external Aether pressure steadies the flow)
- **Capture field** catches leaked Aether before it disperses, stores in glove's small reserve
- Together: ~95% leak prevented + small tactical reserve for combat techniques
- Result: Extended survival from ~14 years → ~17 years (Luna gets 5 additional years)

Black, elegant, seamless integration. Life support disguised as fashion. She has variants for every situation (waterproof, formal, casual), but the black ones are constant. When people ask, she deflects: *"Hmm~? Fashion, obviously! An engineer needs her signature look~"* Clean. Charming. No seam showing.

The gloves allow temporary removal (gradual decay over 15 min - 2 hours). Luna can bathe, perform monthly maintenance, but cannot sleep without them or remove for extended periods. Vixia checks them every night while she pretends to sleep.

### Where Her Ability to Read People Came From

A bedridden child watches. She had nothing else to do. Visitors came and went — military friends of her parents, doctors, teachers, eventually classmates when she could attend school in fragments. She watched how people's faces changed before they spoke. How their voices tightened when they were about to leave. How adults smiled differently when they were lying about "getting better."

She learned to read departure before it happened. She learned to sense where conversations were heading three steps ahead. She learned that if you gave people the right kind of warmth — bright, charming, unthreatening — they stayed satisfied without going deeper.

By the time she was healthy enough to attend Academy regularly, the skill was instinct. She didn't build the koakuma mask deliberately. It grew from years of watching, reading, and surviving. The charm is genuine. Using that charm as defense is so deeply wired it's become invisible even to her.

### Current Status

Luna attends the Royal Academy in the same grade as Vixia despite being younger. She's recognized as an engineering prodigy. The black gloves are constant. Her Crystal Hollow extended survival gives her approximately 5 years remaining (from age ~12 to ~17 with gloves). She knows about Vixia's nighttime glove checks (wakes before the door opens, regulates breathing deliberately, performs sleep while he lifts her wrist to check indicators, lies in the dark afterward with heart hammering). She has been pretending she doesn't know for months.

**The Secrets She Carries:**

Luna discovered the full truth late Saga 1 while packing her mother's workshop. The research diary revealed:

**The Diagnosis (What She Learned):**
- Crystal Hollow confirmed, five-year timeline with gloves (16-19 years maximum)
- Vixia received diagnosis from Doctor Maren, chose to lie to protect her
- Mother suspected diagnosis years earlier, had been researching cure texts
- Parents' R&D deployments were cover for accessing Unknown Region cure research
- The final mission that took them MIA: continental defense operation that passed through restricted archives

**The Guilt (What Formed in Ch 9):**
- Realization: Career sacrifices, R&D role, high-risk deployment — all driven by searching for her cure
- Core wound: "I killed them. My body killed them."
- Resolution: "I won't kill Vixia too. I'll hide decline. I'll protect him from following their path."

**The Mutual Deception:**
- Both siblings know the diagnosis truth, neither confronts
- Luna knows Vixia lied, performs normalcy to protect him from guilt
- Vixia thinks Luna doesn't know, performs normalcy to protect her from dread
- Every "I'm fine~" deflection means "I know you know, but we're pretending"

Both parents remain MIA. No recent contact. Luna carries the weight of knowing they died searching for her.

### Erwin and Iruma Archi (Parents) — The Legacy She's Learning Without Knowing

**What Luna Knows:**

Luna knows her parents as "Erwin Archi" and "Iruma Archi" - deployed to the Northern Frontier Operation, now MIA. She remembers her mother as a skilled engineer who taught her workshop fundamentals, temperature regulation, and iterative design philosophy. She remembers her father as tactical and protective, often deployed. She frames them as "good at their jobs" but has no concept of their continental fame.

**The Advanced Engineering Baseline:**

Luna was raised with engineering knowledge that surpasses typical Academy curriculum, but she doesn't realize how unusual this is:

| What She Learned at Home | Her Rationalization | The Reality |
|--------------------------|---------------------|-------------|
| Thermal cascading techniques, Aether flow regulation, iterative calibration methods | "Mom taught me basics, I'm just applying them" | She's using Suryani's pioneering frameworks that aren't taught until third-year advanced courses |
| Professional-grade workshop tools, precision alignment matrices | "Mom must've had hand-me-downs from someone skilled?" | These were tools used by the lead engineer of Luminar Rails project |
| Temperature regulation for Aether-powered systems | "Common engineering knowledge, right?" | This is Suryani's revolutionary contribution to continental infrastructure |

**What She Doesn't Know:**

- Her mother's professional name: Lead Engineer Iruma Suryani (Luminar Rails pioneer, revolutionary magic-engineer celebrated across Human Federation)
- Her father's professional name: Commander Erwin Herman (3rd Rank Resonator, Strike Corps spearhead who broke the Siege of Mirstone)
- When engineering instructors reference "Suryani's methodology" or "Suryani's approach to thermal cascading," Luna is hearing her mother's name without recognizing it
- Her techniques are being compared to published work by her own mother

**The Instructor Recognition Pattern:**

Luna experiences a recurring pattern she can't fully explain:

1. **Instructor reviews her engineering project**
2. **Instructor comments:** "This approach... this is Suryani's framework. Where did you learn this?"
3. **Luna responds:** "My mother taught me. She was an engineer."
4. **Instructor reaction:** Surprise, re-evaluation, "Your mother must have been quite skilled. This methodology isn't taught until advanced years."
5. **Luna's confusion:** *Why do they keep reacting like Mom's techniques are special? Isn't this standard knowledge?*

She rationalizes: "Mom probably studied Suryani's published work really thoroughly. That's why my methods match."

The truth she doesn't know: She's not studying Suryani's work second-hand. She learned directly from Suryani. The pioneer is her mother.

**The Workshop Tools Moment:**

Early Academy enrollment, Luna examined workshop equipment and noticed: "These precision matrices are cruder than Mom's. The alignment calibration is off by at least two degrees."

She didn't voice this (sounds arrogant, doesn't want to seem uppity). Internal rationalization: "Maybe Mom's tools were hand-me-downs from someone really skilled? Or she modified them herself? I probably just got used to better equipment."

The reality: Her mother's tools were professional-grade because Iruma Suryani WAS the professional whose work defined continental engineering standards.

**The Dramatic Irony:**

Luna sits in engineering lectures learning about "Lead Engineer Suryani's revolutionary Luminar Rails contribution" and takes notes on the techniques. Later, she applies those same techniques in her projects because "Mom taught me this approach." Instructors notice the similarity and comment on it. Luna thinks it's coincidence - that her mother studied the same pioneer everyone references.

She doesn't know she's carrying her mother's legacy forward, that every time an instructor says "your work reminds me of Suryani," they're seeing the direct transmission from parent to child of engineering philosophy that changed a continent.

## Design Notes

### Visual Design

Luna's visual design balances engineering practicality with koakuma charm, where every element serves both aesthetic appeal and thematic purpose.

**Hair:**
- **Style:** Half updo with layered long hair
- **Color:** Soft gradient from black at the roots to sky blue at the tips
- **Thematic reasoning:** 
  - Half updo keeps hair out of workshop work (engineering practicality) while maintaining elegant presentation (social butterfly)
  - Black-to-sky-blue gradient is a *visible consequence* of Crystal Hollow — high Aether density from the structural leak causes bluish discoloration in the hair
  - The gradient shows her condition without being overtly tragic; it's beautiful but hints at something wrong underneath
  - As the leak worsens across sagas, the blue saturation could deepen (visual progression of decline)

**Eyes:**
- **Color:** Clear blue like stars
- **Pupils:** Red
- **Thematic reasoning:**
  - Striking, memorable, slightly unusual — fits the "little devil" archetype
  - Clear blue suggests transparency (ironic given her one-way mirror dynamic)

**Standard Outfit (Academy):**
- **Uniform:** Contect dependent, liked color palette is black/white/blue
- **Gloves:** Black, elegant, seamless integration (life support disguised as fashion)
- **Stockings:** Black
- **Thematic reasoning:**
  - Black gloves = signature look that's always present, never removed in public (literally life support)
  - Black accessories ground the elegant presentation with practical elements
  - Color palette (black/white/blue) is clean, memorable, visually distinct

**Overall Aesthetic:**
- **Elegant but practical** — Can work in a workshop without sacrificing social presentation
- **Koakuma charm** — Bright, approachable, playfully stylish
- **Engineering signature** — Black gloves become her "brand" (people recognize her by them)

### Narrative Function

Luna serves as **emotional core and relationship mirror** — her ability to read people makes her the group's social anchor while her complete transparency around Vixia creates the story's central relationship dynamic. She demonstrates how genuine warmth can be both connection and defense, and how the person who reads everyone can be completely blind to herself.

**Thematic role:** The girl who manages the world and falls apart for him. The koakuma whose charm is both truth and armor.

### Archetype Notes

**Primary archetype:** Koakuma (Little Devil)

**Key manifestations:**
- Genuine brightness as both connection and controlled access
- Playful teasing that never crosses into cruelty
- Social mastery that appears effortless
- One-way mirror effect (people see warmth, don't realize they're being managed)

**Archetype subversion:** Unlike traditional koakuma characters whose charm is pure performance, Luna's warmth is *real*. The manipulation isn't in faking emotion — it's in controlling how deep anyone gets.

### Trust Ceiling

**Most people reach:** L2 (Acquaintance) — think they know her, actually know surface only

**Main cast can reach:** L3 (Comfortable) with sustained interaction, L4 (Trusted) possible but rare

**Only Vixia reaches:** L5 (Intimate) — full access, no walls, ability to read people stops working

### Romance Ceiling

**Vixia specifically:** Romantic Awareness developing (L6 threshold) — feelings exist but uncategorized, no framework to understand them yet

**All others:** Boundary Protection activates immediately — careful warm exits, zero openings created

### Author Notes

**Writing Luna requires tracking TWO distinct characters:**

1. **Public Luna:** Socially masterful, smooth deflections, graceful exits, handles everyone
2. **Luna with Vixia:** Transparent mess, stumbling words, physical clinginess, obvious lies

The contrast MUST be visible when both appear in same scene. Luna doesn't notice the difference. Readers should.

**Critical guidelines:**

- Her deflections with others should feel smooth, almost invisible
- Her attempts at deflection with Vixia should be transparently failing
- With Vixia: NEVER use conversation closers alone, ALWAYS add hook
- Teasing = love language (translate: "Nii-san's hopeless" = "I'm glad you're here")
- Physical contact with Vixia is unconscious (she doesn't acknowledge it, denies if noticed)
- Reading people means confident reads with hedged certainty in internal monologue ("If X, then Y" valid for her)
- Complete blind spot: herself (confusion about own emotions, especially regarding Vixia)

**The nighttime check scene:** Luna knows. She wakes before he enters. She regulates breathing. She performs sleep perfectly. She feels him lift her wrist, check indicators, set her hand back. She lies in the dark afterward, face in pillow, heart hammering. This happens every night. She has been pretending for months. Do not break this pattern without narrative justification.

**Ice manifestation deployment:** When Luna uses ice techniques instead of generic, it's a CHARACTER CHOICE with WEIGHT. She's depleting her glove reserve 2-3x faster, accepting higher life cost for power. The decision should feel significant. The glove reserve refills daily from captured leak, but depleting it in combat means she's using Aether she can't recover mid-fight.

**The Pile:** Luna continuously files observations (Vixia anomalies, Solenne patterns, social dynamics) but doesn't examine them. "I don't look at the pile." This is defense mechanism. She COULD look. She CHOOSES not to. Respect this until narrative forces the examination.
